# Shilevi Product Requirements

## Divisions
Products; Services; Transport & Delivery; Property & Places.

## Provider activity
One account may apply for multiple activities. Each activity has its own application, evidence, review and approval. Approval in one activity does not authorize another. Restricted listings cannot be published without the relevant approved activity.

## Customer and provider
Customers browse/search/filter, review listings, agree exact totals, order/book, track progress, communicate, report issues and raise disputes. Providers manage approved listings, prices, availability, quotes and order status.

## Delivery
Agree product price, delivery fee and total first. Eligible nearby riders receive limited offer information. Seller chooses an eligible interested rider; assignment must be atomic. Reveal exact pickup/destination only to assigned rider when needed. Track only during active delivery. Confirm handover with PIN or appropriate proof. Rating is optional and never gates completion or payout. Handle cancellations, failed delivery, no-shows and safety events.

## Finance
Target standard commission: 10% on a clearly defined, disclosed base. Rules are versioned and effective-dated. Separate item price, delivery fee, commission, taxes, provider proceeds, rider proceeds, refunds and payouts. Use integer minor units, an auditable ledger, idempotency and reconciliation. Do not assume ordinary company funds can be held as escrow; confirm provider capabilities and Kenyan legal requirements.

## Admin
Users, sessions, roles, staff, provider applications, categories, listings, orders, deliveries, payments/reconciliation, commissions, refunds/payouts, disputes, moderation, promotions, service areas, integrations, feature flags, support, analytics, fraud/security and audit logs. Owner access is restricted server-side to two configured emails; fail closed until supplied and verified.

## Trust
Prepare Terms, Privacy, cookie controls, refund/cancellation/dispute policy, provider terms, rider safety rules, prohibited listings, fee disclosures and support contact. Require legal review before launch.
