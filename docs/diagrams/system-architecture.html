3. Money and settlement

Collection → segregated settlement → commission / payout / refund
Customer paysM-PESA STK push or bank / card through the PSP
→
PSP collectionLicensed provider verifies and confirms the payment
→
Segregated settlementPSP / bank account. Not the XYZ operating account.
→
XYZ commission (10% default)→ operating
Provider payout→ M-PESA / bank
Refund→ customer
Holding funds, delayed payout, and any escrow-like behaviour depend on the licensed provider and Kenyan legal requirements. Until that is signed off, launch transaction categories as enquiry-only. “10%” is confirmed; the base, payer, rounding, and low-ticket floor still need a written rule.

A customer cannot withhold payment by refusing to rate, and a provider cannot demand a rating to complete a job. Disputes are the mechanism for withholding money, not stars.
